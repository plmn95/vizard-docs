---
title: "Load a Factory Patch"
description: "Load a factory patch and save a copy."
---

Open `File > Factory Patches` and choose a patch, for example "Big Brother".

Select a module in `Chain` and drag one of its numeric controls in `Module`.
Hold `Shift` while dragging for finer adjustment.

Choose `File > Save As...` or press `Ctrl+Shift+S` (`Cmd+Shift+S` on
macOS). Save your copy as `First factory patch.hxc` in a folder you can
find again. Use `File > Open...` to return to it after building the next
patch.

Factory patches are also available from a patch slot's right-click menu.
