Hex Composer is a video synthesizer you play. This manual documents the app
as it exists now.

## Start here

[Make and record your first moving pattern](getting-started/): load a factory
patch, build a kaleidoscope, animate it with an LFO, and record a short clip.

## Choose a task

- [Make visuals react to audio](reference/modulation/audio-reactive/): choose
  an audio source and assign its level or frequency bands to a parameter.
- [Control a parameter with MIDI](concepts/midi-and-opensoundcontrol-learn/):
  connect a controller and learn an assignment.
- [Create feedback trails](reference/modules/feedback/): start with a recipe
  for repeats, afterimages, or painted trails.
- [Record or send live output](getting-started/04-record-and-output/): save
  a clip or send the output to another application.
- [Troubleshoot a problem](troubleshooting/): check recording, controller
  input, and external output by symptom.
