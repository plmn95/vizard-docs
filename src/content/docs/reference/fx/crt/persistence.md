---
title: "Persistence"
---

Simulates phosphor afterglow: bright content lingers and decays instead of
updating instantly frame to frame.

| Parameter | Control | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amount | Trough | 0 to 1 | 0 fully bypasses the effect. |

The R:G:B decay-time ratio (1.6 : 1.0 : 0.45, matching a real shadow mask
tube's phosphor chemistry) is fixed and not user-exposed.
