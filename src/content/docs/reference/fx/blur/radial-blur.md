---
title: "Radial Blur"
---

Streaks the image outward from a center point, like a zoom or spin blur.

| Parameter | Control | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Center X / Center Y | Pad | -0.5 to 0.5 | |
| Strength | Trough | 0 to 1 | |
