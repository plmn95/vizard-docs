---
title: "Channel Select"
---

Works on the image's channels directly: view one in grayscale, route it to
alpha, or isolate it and zero the rest.

| Parameter | Control | Options | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Channel | Selector | Red, Green, Blue, Alpha | UI-only; not modulation-assignable. |
| Mode | Selector | Grayscale View, Route To Alpha, Isolate Channel | UI-only; not modulation-assignable. |

No continuous parameters.

Related: [Insert FX Chains](../../../../concepts/insert-fx-chains/).
