---
title: "VHS"
---

Layers analog tape artifacts over the image: chroma bleed and horizontal
scan shift, in addition to a base intensity.

| Parameter | Control | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intensity | Trough | 0 to 2 | |
| Bleed | Trough | 0 to 2 | Chroma smear. |
| Scan Shift | Trough | 0 to 2 | Horizontal line jitter. |
