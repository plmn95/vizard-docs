---
title: "Contrast"
---

Increases or decreases contrast around mid-gray.

| Parameter | Control | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amount | Trough | -1 to 2 | -1 flattens to solid mid-gray, 0 is pass-through, 2 is a hard linear punch. |
