---
title: "White Balance"
---

Warm/cool and green/magenta color correction.

| Parameter | Control | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Temperature | Trough | -1 to 1 | Negative cools toward blue, positive warms toward orange. |
| Tint | Trough | -1 to 1 | Negative shifts toward green, positive toward magenta. |
