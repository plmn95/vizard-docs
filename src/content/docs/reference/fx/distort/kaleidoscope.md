---
title: "Kaleidoscope"
---

Mirrors the image into a wedge and repeats it around the center.

| Parameter | Control | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Segments | Trough | 2 to 16 | Number of mirrored wedges. |
| Rotation | Wheel | -180 to 180 | Rotates the mirrored wedges; 180 is a half turn. |
