---
title: "Transform"
---

Offsets, scales, and rotates the whole image.

| Parameter | Control | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offset X / Offset Y | Pad | -1 to 1 | |
| Scale X / Scale Y | Pad | -0.9 to 3 | A Link ties Y to X's position. |
| Rotate | Wheel | -180 to 180 | Rotates around the image center; 180 is upside down. |

Related: [Insert FX Chains](../../../../concepts/insert-fx-chains/).
